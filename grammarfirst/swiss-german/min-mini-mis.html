min, mini, mis

Possessives agree with gender: masculine 'min', feminine and plural 'mini', neuter 'mis'.

min Vatter · mini Mueter · mis Auto · mini Chind · din / dini / dis · sin / sini / sis · üse / üsi / üses

Examples

Das isch min Brüeder.This is my brother.
Mini Schwöschter wohnt z Bärn.My sister lives in Bern.
Wo isch dis Handy?Where is your phone?

Tip: 'z Bärn' means in Bern. With place names people often say 'z' instead of 'in'.

More: The possessives follow the same pattern: masculine without ending (min), feminine and plural with -i (mini), neuter with -s (mis). In the dative: mim Vatter, minere Mueter, mim Chind. 'sin' (his) and 'ire' (her) work the same way.
