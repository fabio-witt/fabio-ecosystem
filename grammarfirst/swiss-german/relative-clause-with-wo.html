Swiss German grammar · Unit 4 · Fäll und Sätz (cases and sentences) · A2

Relative clause with wo

For all relative clauses you use 'wo', whether person or thing, masculine or feminine.

de Maa, wo dete staat · d Frau, wo singt · s Buech, wo ich lise
Standard GermanZurich German
der Mann, der kommtde Maa, wo chunnt
die Frau, die ich kenned Frau, wo-n-ich kenne
das Buch, das ich leses Buech, wo-n-ich lise
die Leute, mit denen ich arbeited Lüüt, wo-n-ich mit ine schaffe

Examples

Das isch de Maa, wo mir ghulfe hät.That is the man who helped me.
D Frau, wo dete wohnt, isch nett.The woman who lives there is nice.
S Restaurant, wo mir gange sind, isch guet gsi.The restaurant we went to was good.

Tip: 'wo' replaces der, die, das, welcher: very simple.

More: 'wo' replaces all relative pronouns: der, die, das, welcher, dem. With a preposition you use 'wo' and add the preposition at the end: 'S Huus, wo-n-ich drin wohne' (the house I live in). The -n- is a linking sound before a vowel.

Quick check

  1. D Lüüt, ___ do schaffed, sind nett. wo / die / wer
    Show answer

    wo

  2. S Auto, ___ ich gchauft han, isch rot. das / wo / was
    Show answer

    wo

  3. De Film, ___ mir gseh händ.
    Show answer

    wo

  4. Translate: The man who is coming.
    Show answer

    De Maa wo chunnt
