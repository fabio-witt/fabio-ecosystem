FAQ

Do I really need Indonesian in Bali?

You can get by with English in Canggu and Ubud, but a few words of Bahasa Indonesia get you better prices at the warung, friendlier landlords, smoother scooter rentals and real conversations with locals.

Is Indonesian hard to learn?

It is one of the easiest Asian languages: Latin alphabet, no tones, no verb conjugation. Most learners can handle daily situations after a few weeks.

What about Balinese?

Lingora teaches Bahasa Indonesia, the national language everybody in Bali speaks. You will also pick up a few Balinese greetings and cultural notes along the way.

How much does it cost?

The course, exams and certificates are free. Lingora Plus is optional.
